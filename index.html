Daofu Zhang

Ph.D. Student, Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Utah

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Utah, where I work with Prof. Fuheng Zhao in the UtahDB lab. My research sits between statistical inference and machine learning systems. I study how to route requests between language models of different cost and capability under a budget, and how adversaries can break the false discovery rate guarantees of distributed and conformal testing procedures.

Before Utah, I received an M.S. in Data Science and Machine Learning from UC San Diego and a B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering with First Class Honors from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).

Research Interests

My work asks when machine learning systems can keep their guarantees, on cost or on error rates, under tight budgets and in the presence of adversaries.

  1. LLM model routing and cost-efficient inference

    Deciding which language model should answer each request under a cost budget, for LLM-powered data processing and agent workloads. I build routers that adapt to a new task and model pair from about 50 paired examples, work in both batch and streaming settings, and come with guarantees on spending.

    ARoute · PVLDB 2027 (submitted)
  2. Conformal inference and adversarially robust novelty detection

    Novelty detection with finite-sample false discovery rate control built on conformal p-values from ML models, such as AdaDetect and one-class classifiers. I study how adversarial perturbations can break these guarantees while detection power stays high, and what that implies for robust alternatives.

    Conformal novelty detection · under review
  3. Multiple testing and FDR control in distributed systems

    Large-scale hypothesis testing across distributed nodes with false discovery rate control, when some nodes are Byzantine and report corrupted p-values. I characterize how such attacks inflate the global FDR and design counter-attacks that restore control.

    Byzantine multiple testing · ISIT 2025

Publications

Under Review

  1. PVLDB 2027Submitted
    ARoute: Task-Aligned Batch and Streaming Model Routing
    Daofu Zhang, Qingcheng Zeng, Chunwei Liu, Fuheng Zhao
    Submitted to Proceedings of the VLDB Endowment (PVLDB), 2027

    A router that decides, before inference, whether each request goes to a cheaper or a stronger LLM under a cost budget. It has batch and streaming variants, and the streaming variant comes with a finite-sample guarantee on expected normalized spending. ARoute reaches the highest mean accuracy in all 15 task–cost settings and finishes a batch up to 2.8× faster than cascades.

  2. CSTMUnder review
    On the Adversarial Robustness of Learning-based Conformal Novelty Detection
    Daofu Zhang, Mehrdad Pournaderi, Hanne M. Clifford, Yu Xiang, Pramod K. Varshney
    Communications in Statistics – Theory and Methods (under review)

    Black-box attacks that only need label queries can push the false discovery rate of AdaDetect and one-class conformal novelty detection well above its target level while keeping detection power high. We also bound the worst-case FDR degradation.
