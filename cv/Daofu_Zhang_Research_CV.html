Research Experience

Ph.D. Research Assistant — University of Utah
2024 – Present
Task-Aligned Model Routing for LLM Workloads (ARoute) [S2]
2026
  • Proposed ARoute, a router that learns per-request accuracy-gain and incremental-cost estimates to choose between a weaker and a stronger LLM before inference, adapting to a new task and model pair from about 50 paired records.
  • Designed two variants: ARoute-Batch assigns the stronger model to the requests with the greatest expected benefit under a cost budget; ARoute-Stream routes online with a validation-calibrated threshold that adjusts as the budget is consumed. Proved a finite-sample guarantee on expected normalized spending for ARoute-Stream.
  • Evaluated on MMLU-Pro, LiveCodeBench, SuperGPQA, and two SWAN value-filling tasks against RouteLLM, FrugalGPT, BARGAIN, and OctoSelector: highest mean accuracy in all 15 task–cost settings (+4.1 points on average, up to +11.9) and up to 2.8× faster batch completion than cascades.
Adversarial Robustness of Conformal Novelty Detection [S1]
2025 – 2026
Supported in part by NSF Grant CCF-2611415
  • Studied two learning-based conformal novelty detection frameworks with finite-sample FDR control (AdaDetect and a one-class classifier approach), analyzing how their ML-based empirical p-values behave under adversarial perturbation.
  • Formulated an oracle attack and derived an upper bound on the worst-case FDR degradation; turned it into a practical attack that needs only query access to output labels.
  • Combined the attack with two black-box adversarial algorithms on synthetic and real-world data, showing that FDR can be pushed well above its target level while detection power stays high.
Distributed Multiple Testing under Byzantine Attacks [C1]
2024 – 2025
Supported in part by NSF Grant CCF-2420146
  • Theorized and empirically evaluated distributed multiple testing with FDR control when captured nodes report corrupted p-values.
  • Characterized the effect on global FDR of two attacks: one with full knowledge of the true nulls, and one that infers nulls with the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure.
  • Designed counter-attack strategies that restore FDR guarantees.
